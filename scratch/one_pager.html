1. Executive Simple Explanation — What Does This App Do?
ErgoSafe Reborn V3 is an AI-powered Workplace Ergonomics, Driver Fatigue Telemetry, and OHS Regulatory Compliance Platform. Built to act as an active digital safety officer, it monitors worker posture in office/hybrid setups, detects long-haul driver shift exhaustion in real time, and maintains zero-knowledge audit trails that insulate corporate directors from statutory legal liability under South African occupational safety laws.
⚡ 3D Biomechanical Posture Telemetry

Using spatial vision & 3D spine models (Nelly Engine), it tracks cervical/lumbar spine alignment. Detects tech-neck flexion (cervical load jumping from 12 lbs to 60 lbs), slouching, and couch/bed risk states.

🚛 Shandray's Prizm Driver Fatigue Engine

Monitors continuous driving hours and reaction time drops. Automatically computes fatigue risk scores and issues mandatory rest alerts before transport fatigue incidents occur.

⚖️ Section 37 OHS Legal & Audit Ledger

Automatically logs posture breaches, unverified hazards, and driver alerts into an administrative compliance dossier, fulfilling strict Section 37 requirements of the OHS Act 85 of 1993.

🗣️ Multilingual Nelly Voice Assistant

Provides real-time voice triage and postural coaching across 7 regional languages (isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, English, German, Mandarin, KiSwahili) with localized speech accents.

🛡️ Behavior-Based Safety (BBS) Micro-Interventions

When hazards trigger, the app instantly delivers interactive corrective overlays: 15-second postural reset stretches, 60-second driver power-breathing breaks, and 20-20-20 ocular glides. Completion is verified and logged.

Voice Triage Engine Web Speech API (src/utils/speech.ts) Bi-directional speech synthesis & recognition with pitch/rate modulation and multi-locale speech routing.
